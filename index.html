Welcome

Discover Lithuania

If you really want to know Lithuania, you must first begin to get to know its people. Most Lithuanians are very sincere, hospitable and helpful — and, in fact, we expect the same treatment in return from our guests.

Lithuania is a country of seemingly endless forests, lakes scattered across its landscape, rivers that criss-cross the country, and the unforgettably beautiful Curonian Spit bathed by the Baltic Sea.

Whether you are drawn by its medieval old towns, its vibrant startup scene, or the warmth of its people — Lithuania awaits. We are pleased to welcome citizens of Cameroon and West Africa to explore everything this remarkable nation has to offer.

About

About Lithuania

A proud Baltic nation with a rich history, vibrant culture, and a growing presence on the global stage.

🏛️

History & Heritage

Lithuania is one of Europe's oldest nations, with a history stretching back over a millennium. Its Old Town of Vilnius is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, deeply rooted in language, folklore, and traditions.

🇪🇺

EU & NATO Member

A member of the European Union and NATO since 2004, Lithuania is a stable and growing democracy at the heart of the Baltic region, with strong rule of law and a business-friendly environment.

💻

Digital Economy

Lithuania has one of Europe's fastest-growing fintech sectors and has attracted over 200 licensed financial institutions. It offers a highly skilled workforce, competitive costs, and full EU market access.

🌍

Global Relations

Lithuania maintains strong diplomatic ties worldwide and is committed to international development cooperation, contributing to peace and sustainable growth in partner countries across Africa and beyond.

Our Representative

The Honorary Consul & Team

Meet the team dedicated to strengthening ties between Lithuania and Cameroon.

DS

Deli Sarsar

Honorary Consul of the Republic of Lithuania in Cameroon

Mr. Deli Sarsar serves as the Honorary Consul of the Republic of Lithuania in Cameroon, appointed to represent Lithuanian interests and strengthen bilateral relations between both nations. Based in Yaoundé, he is the sole official representative of Lithuania in the country.

The Honorary Consulate is based behind the Mbn Global Education campus in Yaoundé. Entrance at the back. Mr. Sarsar works to connect Cameroonian students and professionals with international academic opportunities, including pathways to study in Lithuania and other EU member states.

Additionally, Mr. Sarsar leads Prime Nexus, a technology firm that partners with businesses and institutions worldwide to design, secure, and operate technology meeting international standards — specialising in cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, data management, and software delivery.

Through these combined roles, Mr. Sarsar actively promotes cultural exchange, educational partnerships, technology cooperation, and economic ties between Lithuania and Cameroon.

Partnership

Lithuanian Development Cooperation

Lithuania is committed to supporting sustainable development globally through official development assistance.

Lithuania implements development cooperation through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and in partnership with UN agencies, EU institutions, and bilateral partners. Key focus areas include democracy, education, capacity building, and civil society support.

The Baltic Development Cooperation initiative brings together Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania in joint projects, contributing to the EU's collective development assistance framework.

Strategy of the Baltic Sea Region

The EU Strategy for the Baltic Sea Region (EUSBSR) promotes cooperation on environment, transport, energy, and economic development. Lithuania is an active participant and champion of this regional framework.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the consulate and visiting Lithuania.

An Honorary Consul is an unpaid representative appointed to perform limited consular functions. They cannot issue passports or Schengen visas but can provide information, emergency assistance, notarial services, and act as a liaison between the two countries.

Cameroonian nationals require a Schengen visa. Applications must be submitted at the Embassy of Lithuania in Cairo, Egypt, or in Pretoria, South Africa. The Consulate provides guidance and documentation advice but does not process visa applications.

Yes. Lithuanian citizens in distress should contact the Consulate immediately. We assist with emergency liaison to Lithuanian authorities, including coordination with the Embassies in Cairo and Pretoria, and can facilitate emergency travel documentation.

The Lithuanian Government offers state scholarships for students from developing countries including Cameroon. Many universities also offer their own international scholarships. Contact the Consulate or visit Mbn Global Education for current opportunities.

Key areas include agriculture, IT, renewable energy, education services, healthcare, and technology. Lithuania excels in fintech, laser technology, cybersecurity (via Prime Nexus), and advanced manufacturing. The Consulate facilitates business introductions.

Appointments are strongly recommended. Please contact us by phone or email during office hours to schedule. Walk-ins may be received subject to availability.

Cameroon is a signatory to the Hague Apostille Convention. Documents must be apostilled by the Cameroonian Ministry of Justice. The Consulate can guide you through the full process.
